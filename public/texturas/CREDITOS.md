# Real textures of Earth, the Moon and Mars

They are generated by `node scripts/texturas-reales.mjs` from public domain images from NASA
and USGS. The downloads go to `.cache/texturas-reales/` (outside git). Only the reduced web
versions are kept here. The client knows what is available from `src/mundo/planetas/texturasReales.json`.

## What was changed

- **Earth by day:** reduced to 4096×2048. The sea is shifted toward a dark navy blue using the
  water mask, because the mosaic paints the bathymetry, which cannot be seen from orbit. Very
  shallow waters, like the Bahamas, keep part of their turquoise. This is a visual choice.
- **Night lights:** luminance only. The gray background from ice and terrain is cut out. The
  sodium lamp tint is added by the shader.
- **Clouds:** in gray, with a bit more contrast. They are clouds from a satellite composite,
  not the clouds on the day of the mission.
- **Relief:** east and north slopes of the elevation model, stored as 128 + slope × 300
  in the red and green channels. The shader exaggerates them so they show up at this resolution.
- They all use the same projection: equirectangular, column 0 at longitude -180°, north up.

The SVS Moon color maps are made to look good, not for science.
The site itself says so.
